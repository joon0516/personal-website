Hi, I'm Joon. I’m a first-year CS PhD student at Princeton University, where I'm advised by Lydia T. Liu. I did my undergrad at University of Virginia, where I studied computer science and math and worked with Ferdinando Fioretto. I also spent a wonderful summer in 2025 as a research intern at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Steven Wu.

I work on the societal impact of AI, particularly in high-stakes decision-making settings. I'm interested in algorithmic fairness, differential privacy, optimization, and uncertainty quantification. My research is supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. Fun fact: my Erdős number is 3.
